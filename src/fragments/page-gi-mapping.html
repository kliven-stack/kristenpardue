Why I love the GI-MAP Test?

The GI Microbial Assay Plus (or GI-MAP™) test provides an extremely detailed analysis of your gut microbiome via stool testing. This is a unique and targeted testing option, playing a critical role in knowing what YOU specifically need for YOUR body. Since your microbiome is the population of the bacteria, viruses, and potential parasites residing in the gut, we can detail out what is going on and pin point a CUSTOMIZED PLAN OF ACTION!

What are we looking for?

This test analyzes the levels of both good and bad bacteria, parasites, viruses, worms, and fungi, as well as immune and digestive markers in your gut. Knowing how much of an organism’s DNA is present in the gut is VITAL information and also gives us a map to figure out inflammation markers.

Getting a GI-MAP allows for a one-of-a-kind, personalized treatment plan and allows retesting options to track progress of your Gut Health journey!

Everyone benefits from analyzing their gut health!

This test gives us SO MUCH important data, even the “healthiest of people” can find ways to optimize their digestive health! 

Adults AND children can benefit from these tests.

I even recommend families considering running it together, as many of these pathogens can be passed among in families or significant others and by addressing them together, the entire family can feel better and be proactive about their long-term health. WIN-WIN!

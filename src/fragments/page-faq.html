Can I use essential oils on my baby?

Absolutely! I’ve been using doTERRA oils on my babies since the day I birthed them. The main thing is diluting them with approximately ½ tablespoon of coconut oil (or any carrier oil) since babies’ skin are more sensitive. 

I’m pregnant…what oils do I need to avoid and which ones can I use?

I highly recommend getting Stephanie Fritz’s book “Essential Oils for Pregnancy, Birth, & Babies.” (link to her book in my amazon shop) It will be SO helpful to you.

But pregnancy is a time when these oils will help with so many different things such as nausea (morning sickness), digestive issues, constipation, immune support, uplifting mood, back pain, and more!

Help for my Thinning Hair!

I WANT MY HAIR BACK! 🤪

I’ve had a TON of people message me about the story I posted with these two awesome oils, so figured I’d do a post on them as well! 😘👍 Many people may or may not know that one of the super fun (often not discussed) post partum realities is HAIR LOSS / THINNING HAIR! 🥺😩🥴 So I’ve been determined to bring in more natural options into my daily routine to encourage a healthy scalp and hair growth! Luckily, dōTERRA’s CEDARWOOD and ROSEMARY are an AWESOME COMBO for this! 👩🏼💕✨

I simply put 3-4 drops of each oil in the palm of my hand and then rub on to my scalp and leave on for 10 minutes before I shower…and THAT’S IT! 🥳 You may prefer a spray or dropper bottle with a carrier oil for ease, so whatever works best for you! And the longer you can leave on your scalp—overnight even—the better!

Rosemary is known as one of the best oils for enhancing hair growth and thickening hair (as well as increasing cellular metabolism that stimulates the hair growth in the first place!) Cedarwood can help stimulate the hair follicles by increasing circulation to the scalp. HOW COOL IS THAT?! An EASY and NATURAL way we can combat hair loss?! YES, PLEASE! 🙋🏼‍♀️
